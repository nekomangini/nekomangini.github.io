Animation Log 06

June 9, 2024

Stick figure animation

In this animation practice, I challenged myself by animating a punch animation involving two characters. Throughout the process, I encountered several challenges. Firstly, animating two characters simultaneously proved to be more complex than anticipated. Secondly, accurately capturing the impact and follow-through animations of the punch posed additional difficulties. To tackle these challenges, I employed a combination of layered animation and straight-ahead animation techniques.

What I learned:

  • Complexities involved in animating interactions between multiple characters.
  • Techniques for managing character interactions and maintaining continuity.
  • Improved understanding of timing and spacing in impact animations.
  • Challenges in achieving fluidity and realism in punch animations.
  • Skills in integrating layered and straight-ahead animation approaches.

Problems Encountered:

  • Difficulty in synchronizing movements between two characters.
  • Challenges in depicting the physical impact and follow-through of the punch.
  • Complexity in managing layered animation sequences.

This practice has deepened my appreciation for the intricacies of character animation and the importance of meticulous planning and execution. Moving forward, I aim to refine my skills in character interaction and continue exploring effective animation techniques.
