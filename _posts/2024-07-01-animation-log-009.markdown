What I learned:

- How to create multiple versions of animations using the NLA editor.
- Techniques for extending animations and managing action sequences.
- The importance of experimentation to explore different animation outcomes.

Problems Encountered:

- Difficulty in seamlessly combining two action sequences in the NLA editor.
- Initial challenges in understanding the best practices for action sequence integration.
- Time spent troubleshooting and resolving issues with overlapping actions.

Overall, this practice was both enjoyable and educational. It deepened my understanding of the NLA editor and highlighted areas for improvement in managing complex action sequences. Moving forward, I plan to refine my approach to combining actions to enhance the fluidity and coherence of my animations.
