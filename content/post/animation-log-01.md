### Ball animation

As you can see in the video below, during my recent animation project involving a soft ball, I encountered an issue with the bounce dynamics. The ball's movement lacked the desired degree of realism and elasticity, which detracted from the overall impact of the animation.

To address this, I plan to extend the animation duration. By allowing more time for the ball to interact with its environment and demonstrate a more pronounced bounce, I aim to achieve a smoother and more lifelike portrayal of its physical properties.

This adjustment aligns with my commitment to refining animation techniques and enhancing visual storytelling through meticulous attention to detail.
