A small Flutter app I built so I would stop hunting through menus for shortcuts. Every
workspace gets its own screen listing the keys and what they do.

Here are some screenshots:

{{< figure src="03.webp" width="360" alt="Modeling shortcut list, showing entries such as Ctrl+B to bevel a selection and P to separate the selection into its own object." >}}
